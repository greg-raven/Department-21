title: "Listening to my body"
author: "Noam Sain"
layout: post
---

They keep telling us to get in touch with our bodies.

Mine isn't all that communicative but I heard from it the other day after I asked, "Body, how'd you like to go to the six o'clock class in vigorous toning?"

Clear as a bell my body said, "listen witch ... do it and die."
